# Everett to Petersen 12-March-1959.pdf

Dr. Aage Petersen Blegdamsvej 17 Copenhagen Ø, Denmark  

Dear Aage:  

I will be leaving here this Sunday, (15th), stay a day in London, then arrive in Copenhagen Tuesday, (17th), 1:30 PM or so. We (Nancy, Liz, and I) plan to stay at the d'Angleterre Hotel for a while at least. I will be in Copenhagen till April 24. Looking forward to many good arguments (not discussions, of course). I love informal discussions but detest formal talks, so please do not arrange any formal seminars.  

See you soon,  

Hugh Everett
