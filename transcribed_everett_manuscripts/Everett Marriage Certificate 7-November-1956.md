# THE STATE OF MARYLAND  

MONTGOMERY COUNTY, SCT.  

TO ALL WHOM IT DOTH OR MAY CONCERN:  

This is to Certify, That on the 7th day of November in the year one thousand nine hundred and fifty- six a Marriage License was issued from the Office of the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Montgomery County authorizing any Minister of the Gospel or any other person authorized by law to perform the Rites of Marriage between  

MALE'S NAME Hugh Everett III Age 25 Birthplace D.C. Residence Alexandria, Va. Marital Status Single  

FEMALE'S NAME Nancy Gordon Gore Age 26 Birthplace Mass. Residence Princeton, New Jersey Marital Status Single Relationship, if any none Applicant Hugh Everett III  

AND AFTERWARDS, to- wit: on the 14th day of November in the year one thousand nine hundred and fifty- six the following certificate was filed in the Clerk's Office of said Court, to- wit:  

"I HEREBY CERTIFY, That on this 10th day of November one thousand nine hundred and fifty- six at Bethesda, Md. Hugh Everett III and Nancy Gordon Gore  

were by me united in Marriage, in accordance with the License issued by the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Montgomery County in the State of Maryland."  

Rev. Nelson E. Brenner Minister of the Gospel, or Authorized Official  

As appears by the Record of Marriages in the Office of the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Montgomery County, in Liber 103.  

IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and  

affixed the seal of the Circuit Court for Montgomery County  

this 26th day of February 19 90  

Clerk of the Circuit Court for Montgomery County
