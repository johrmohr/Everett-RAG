# Telegraph Wheeler to Bohr 26-May-1956.pdf

26 May 1956
Wheeler in Leiden to
N. Bohr in Copenhagen
Telegram, 1 p. E. 

WHEELER, J. A
Bohr Scient. Corr. 

"everett now princeton phoned asking confer with you hopes fly almost immediately but must return in midjune you cable him if inconvenient my great hope thesis suitable danish academy publication after revision have answered Stern regards - John.
