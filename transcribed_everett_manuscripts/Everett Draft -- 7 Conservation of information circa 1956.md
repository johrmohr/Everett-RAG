An intuitively point is the conservation of information.

The natural representation of a point \((x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)\) in the phase space of all points is momentum distribution.

The natural motion of the system then carries each point into another, defining a continuous transformation of the phase space into itself. According to Liouville, then the measure of a set of points of the phase space is invariant with respect to this evolution.
