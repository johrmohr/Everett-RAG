LEIDEN, May 24, 1956. 

Dear Niels: 

Janette and I have spoken many times since our return of how happy we were to be back in Copenhagen, and of the warm hospitality that you and Margrethe gave us. I appreciate more than I can say your kindness in speaking to me so much out of your heart. 

I enclose a little reprint, "A Septet of Sibyls" in which you will see also how I have taken the liberty to mention a few special things out of our past days together. Let me say also how warming it was to have your kind invitation to come to Copenhagen for an extended period, as for example during my next leave of absence in the spring of 1958. That would be a wonderful privilege for me. I will report more closely when the time comes closer and I know my circumstances more fully.

---

I have sent two letters to Hugh Everett about our discussions, including Aage Peterson's comments. I have told Everett that Stern was to report on his thesis and that I would forward him your further written comments as soon as I received them. I also told him that I felt it was important for him to discuss the issues with you directly and arrive at a set of words to describe his formalism that would make sense and be free from misunderstandings for this purpose. I offered to pay his one way fare to Copenhagen out of a special fund that some friends have given me. He may have trouble coming because he does not pass out of the draft age until November, but I hope to hear favorably from him. 

Please tell Margrethe how much I enjoyed seeing and talking with her, and accept all good wishes. 

Yours,
