# Rosenfeld to Belinfante 31-October-1972.pdf

Professor Fred Belinfante  Purdue University  Department of Physics  Lafayette, Indiana 47907  

Dear Belinfante,  

I have now thought over the points raised in your letter of 1 September. With regard to the retrodiction paper, I have nothing to say, except perhaps that although I was much interested by the fuller and more general treatment presented in the new version, I could not repress some slight regret for the preceding version, which was easier to read.  

As to the case of Everett, I see your logical point, and have therefore to grant you unrestricted right to adopt Everett's point of view for the sake of argument, in order to show that it can only be rescued by the quite essential modification you propose. Since this logical situation is (at least to me) rather subtle, I feel that a more explicit mention of it somewhere (just a sentence, but a forceful one) might help the reader and prevent misunderstandings. We have, thus, come to the end of a discussion, which I trust has been fruitful, and which, at any rate, I found most stimulating.  

With heartiest wishes,  

Yours,  

L. Rosenfeld
