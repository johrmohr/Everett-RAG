# Petersen to Wheeler 26-May-1956.pdf

Professor John A. Wheeler, Institut- Lorentz Langebrug lll Leiden.  

Dear Professor Wheeler,  

I am herewith returning Everett's paper and hope that the delay has not caused you inconvenience. As you know, Dr. A. Stern gave a seminar on it, and it would have been nice if you or Everett could have been here to take part in the discussion. Professor Bohr had intended to write you further on the questions discussed in the thesis, but due to his very heavy schedule at the moment, he has not so far found time to do so and tomorrow he will have to go to London for a few days. However, he hopes on his return to write you in more detail about his attitude regarding the epistemological situation in quantum physics and especially about the status of observers in the complementary mode of description.  

Needless to say, we all enjoyed your stay in Copenhagen very much. Betty and I were glad to see you and Mrs. Wheeler again, and especially it was a pleasure to have lunch with you before you left.  

With many greetings, also to Mrs. Wheeler and Misener, whom we hope to see here soon.  

Sinoerely,  

P.W.1 The manuscript is sent by the same A. Petersen. as registered bookpost. With kindest regards both to you and Mrs. Wheeler
