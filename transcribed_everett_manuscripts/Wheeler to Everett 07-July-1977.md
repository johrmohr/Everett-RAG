# Wheeler to Everett 07-July-1977.pdf

Dr. Hugh Everett III  8114 Touchstone Terrace  McLean, Virginia 22101  

Dear Hugh,  

I failed to show you at the time of your most recent welcome visit to Austin the enclosed January 20 and February 14, 1977 correspondence from Paul Benioff of the Argonne National Laboratory along with his preprint "Finite and infinite measurement sequences in quantum mechanics, randomness, and the Everett Wheeler Interpretation". I would welcome your judgment of his conclusions on pages 30 and 32. If it would simplify discussion of these two points or the rest of his paper maybe you would be willing to ring me at High Island 207- 644- 8138, before my July 23 departure for a 3- week trip away. In any case, would you be willing to return the manuscript and letter to me?  

We still think of your visit to Austin as one of the high points of this past Spring. If you see Jan Lodal please give him my best regards. He is a wonderful person. Best regards!
