# PALMER PHYSICAL LABORATORY  Princeton University  PRINCETON, NEW JERSEY  

18 March 1957  

Mr. Hugh Everett  Weapons Systems Evaluation Group  Pentagon  Washington, D.C.  

Dear Hugh:  

Would you be good enough to send your correction to Condon?  Would you also send out with the copies of your thesis to the selected mailing list a copy also of my accompanying article and for that purpose would you please let us know how many copies you want?  

Regards,  

John A. Wheeler  

P. S. Miss Farlee says she won't do anything about setting the date of your exam (which will probably be April 23rd) until you've met all the formal requirements such as the \(\) 40\$ fee, the bound copies of your thesis, etc. Will you notify her when all this is done?

Prof. W. Whelan
