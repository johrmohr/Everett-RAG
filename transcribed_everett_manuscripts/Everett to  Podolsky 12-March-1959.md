# Everett to  Podolsky 12-March-1959.pdf

Professor Boris Podolski University of Cincinnati Department of Physics Cincinnati, Ohio  

Dear Professor Podolski:  

I am sorry to be so late in writing you.  

I am unable to send you the original (large version) of my thesis at this time since I am departing for Copenhagen this Sunday to argue about it with Bohr for a month or so. I will endeavor to send it to you upon my return, however, i? you still want to see it.  

I enjoyed our discussion in New York very much.  

Best Regards,  

Hugh Everett, III
