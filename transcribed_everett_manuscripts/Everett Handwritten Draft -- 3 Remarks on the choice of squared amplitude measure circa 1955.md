While the first right an artificial choice, and one

Let us consider the search for a general
scheme for assigning a measure to the elements of
a superposition of orthogonal states. Let \( \psi_1, \psi_2, \ldots, \psi_n \) be
regime then a positive function \( m \) of the complex

superposition, so that \( m(\psi_i) \) shall be the

that this be general scheme shall be unambiguous,
we must imagine that the states themselves always
be normalized, so that we can distinguish the
coefficients from the states themselves. However,
we can still only determine the coefficients by
an arbitrary phase factor, and hence the function
\( m \) must be a function of the amplitudes of the
coefficients alone, i.e. \( m(\psi_i) = m(\psi_i) \), in order
to avoid ambiguity.
