# Petersen to Everett 11-October-1957.pdf

Dear Hush, 

I am very sorry to have let your long letter and your
short announcement go unannounced for several months. The last
one came soon after 'harlie had told us about your marriage,
and needless to say we were immensely pleased. (When I got your
letter I was of course glad to learn that you may come to Europe.
Now it turns out, however, that we go to the U.S. next week
and stay at MIT until around April. As you may know, both will
give some lectures at MIT during November, and in January we
expect to be in Princeton. I hope that this journey will not
prevent us from seeing each other and I look forward very much
to talking with you again and especially to discussing the
arguments in your letter. 

Both has been here for a few months and we have had many
pleasant discussions. He is working hard on a new interpretation. 

Please write me at MIT about your plans. 

Sincerely, 

Peterson
V. Kirkeraards Alle 22
København SV, Danmark 

Dr. Hush Everett, III
607 Palham Street
Alexandria, Virginia
U.S.A.
