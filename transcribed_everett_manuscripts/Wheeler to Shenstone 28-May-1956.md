# Wheeler to Shenstone 28-May-1956.pdf

Professor A. G. Shenstone  Palmer Physical Laboratory  Princeton, New Jersey.  

Dear Allen:  

This is to ask if you have any objection to my attending the International Conference on Theoretical Physics, Seattle, September 17- 21, the return engagement for the conference held at Japan for w I helped get money and at which I served as a Vice President of the International Union. I expect to be back in Princeton the morning of September 11, and understand Physics 103 begins September 24. I real- that there will be busy days in the interim preparing for the course but I am so impressed with the wonderful job done by McIntosh, Trei and Gerhart in the past that I trust this plan would not present se difficulties. I particularly want to have serious talks with the individual instructors well before the course begins.  

Eugene arranged on his own initiative some time ago about part payment of travel by me, but I had been dubious about going until recently when I discovered that the university begins a week later t I had expected.  

I just received a letter from Miss Farlee asking if I would go again Sept. 20 to the Orange Key group as I had gone with pleasure fall. If you conclude it is appropriate for me to go to Seattle ins would you tell her so?  

After a first review in Copenhagen of Everett's Thesis in its present only partly satisfactory second draft, I have urged him to and struggle it out in person with Bohr for a few weeks. I would like to see the thesis reach a form where it will be accepted for public in the Danish Academy. I think his very original ideas are going to receive wide discussion. I am insistent that the bugs be got out before it gets published, not afterwards. Since the strongest pres opposition to some parts of it comes from Bohr, I feel that accept in the Danish Academy would be the best public proof of having pass the necessary tests. Because of my feeling of the importance of this mutual agreement before publication, I am contributing 260 toward Everett's travel out of my very small Elementary Particle Research Fund.  

Best regards. Sincerely,  

(John A. Wheeler)
