# Rosenfeld to Belinfante 12-October-1972.pdf

Professor Fred Belinfante  Purdue University  Department of Physics  Lafayette, Indiana 47907 

Dear Belinfante, 

Thanks for your letter of 5 October. My failure to react so far to your last letter is due to the fact that when it arrived I had left Copenhagen for a longer trip abroad, from which I have only just returned. I have only cursorily glanced at your letter, and I can already see that I see your point concerning Everett. I must, however, think a bit more about the problem, and I shall write to you next week without fail. 

I am very sorry for the inconvenience that I unwittingly caused you. 

With best wishes, 

Yours, 

L. Rosenfeld
