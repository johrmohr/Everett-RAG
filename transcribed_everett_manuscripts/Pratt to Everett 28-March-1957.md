# Pratt to Everett 28-March-1957.pdf

# PALMER PHYSICAL LABORATORY  Princeton University  PRINCETON, NEW JERSEY  

28 larch 1957  

Mr. Hugh Everett III  Weapons Systems Evaluation Group  Fantagon  Washington, D. C.  

Dear Mr. Everett:  

Professor Wheeler has asked me to tell you that your exam will be at 2.00 pm on April 23rd and also that he has arranged with Professor Morgenstern to have lunch with you and him at the Annex at 12.30 that same day.  

Sincerely,  

Marjory Pratt
