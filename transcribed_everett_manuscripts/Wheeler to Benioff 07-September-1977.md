# Wheeler to Benioff 07-September-1977.pdf

Department of Physics  

Dr. Paul Benioff  Chemistry Division  Argonne National Laboratory  9700 South Cass Avenue  Argonne, IL 60439  

Dear Dr. Benioff:  

Thanks for your letter and the new manuscript. I trust you have sent a copy to Dr. Hugh Everett (8114 Touchstone Terrace, McLean, VA 22101). In this connection, I would be ever so grateful if you would change the reference from Everett- Wheeler to Everett interpretation because the credit goes entirely to him; I was merely a commentator in the following paper in Review of Modern Physics. We are reviewing your manuscript here with great interest and I hope I will be able to write you more shortly.  

Every good wish.
