Cunhuy, August 3, 1957 

Dear Mr. Enright, 

Dear Mr. Enright,
Please excuse my long delay in answering.
You kindly let me know that you have
left the time on a trip abroad and that
we have your letter with us. I am sorry
to convey your letter, but I am sorry
to convey it again. 

Please, I am very sorry that time has
been so busy for you as carefully as it
seems to be without it. The first place you
pursue seems rather attractive to me. I have
always disliked the traditional treatment of
a new customer. I cannot say exactly
what it seems to be, but it seems to me
be at first a fact which is entirely

---

If you fall off the path, Heaven, I shall never run again. I shall return with a new heart. I shall find you pure. I shall be your friend. I shall be your companion. I shall be your friend. I shall be your friend. I shall be your friend. I shall be your friend.

---

Very nice! you
