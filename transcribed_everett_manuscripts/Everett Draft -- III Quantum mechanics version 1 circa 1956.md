For two Hilbert spaces \(E_1\) and \(E_2\), we construct the direct product Hilbert space \(E_1 \otimes E_2 = E_1 \otimes E_2\) (tensor product) which is taken to be the space of all possible sums of formal products of points of \(E_1\) and \(E_2\), i.e. the elements of \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) and there of this form \(\sum a_i \otimes b_i\). The scalar product in \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) is taken to be \((\sum a_i \otimes b_i)(\sum a_j \otimes b_j) = \sum a_i^* a_j b_j^* (b_i | b_j\rangle) (n_i | n_j\rangle)\). This then easily ensures that if \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) form complex orthonormal system in \(E_1\) and \(E_2\) respectively, then the product of all formal products \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) is a complete orthonormal set in \(E_1 \otimes E_2\). For any pair of operators \(A, B\) in \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) and \(E_2\) then corresponding an operator \(C = A \otimes B\), the direct product of \(A \otimes B\) in \(E_1 \otimes E_2\), which can be defined by its effect on the elements \(E_1 \otimes E_2\) of \(E_1 \otimes E_2\): 

\[C(E_1 \otimes E_2) = A \otimes B (E_1 \otimes E_2) = (A \otimes B)(E_1 \otimes E_2)\]
