# Handwritten draft Everett to Petersen 19-February-1958.pdf

Dr. Nage between
To Physica Dept.
MIT 

FL - 15823
2-19-58 

Dear Nage: 

I am very glad to receive
your letter. 

I'm possibly too anxious that
I could spend several weeks in
Spendagen around Tully. Do
you know if this would be a good
time? 

It was my nice to see
you and Betty again, and = hope
to see you both again soon 

- Dr. E.
