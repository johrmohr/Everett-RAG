**Hit Probabilities** \(p(m,n,k)\) = probability of k targets out of a total of n with m weapons

If 

\[F(k,k) = \xi_k\]

\[\hat{p}(m,k) = \sum_{k=0}^{k} \hat{f}(m-\frac{1}{2},k)\]

Then: 

\[p(m,n,k) = \frac{\sum_{k=0}^{k} \hat{f}(m,k) (K_n)}{1 - \sum_{k=0}^{k} \hat{f}(m,k)}\]
