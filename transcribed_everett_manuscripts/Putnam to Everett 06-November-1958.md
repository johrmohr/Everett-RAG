# PALMER PHYSICAL LABORATORY  Princeton University  PRINCETON, NEW JERSEY

Dr. Hugh Everett  WSEQ  Pentagon  Washington, D.C.  

Dear Hugh:  

What of course I should have added in my note is that your point of view seems precisely what is needed to provide a natural physical ground for some of Eddington's arguments, removing the quasi- epistemological confusion and mystery surrounding the status of his approach and giving us a way to try and ground some of such on purely physical reasoning.  

Peter
