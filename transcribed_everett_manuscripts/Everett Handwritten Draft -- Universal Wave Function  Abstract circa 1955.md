# Everett Handwritten Draft -- Universal Wave Function  Abstract circa 1955.pdf

Brief Abstract: It is shown that a theory which regards the basic entity as the invariant function itself, and in which at all times stays a wave equation, (no quantum jumps) can be successfully interpreted to yield a theory in correspondence with experience. The problem of observation is treated entirely within the theory, by assigning state functions to observers, (which are regarded just like all other physical systems) and investigating the subjective appearances to their observers. The result is that the probabilistic aspects of the causal formulation are deduced within the present scheme as subjective appearances to observers. This all probability and discontinuous phenomena (quantum jumps) exist only at a subjective level within a theory which is objectively continuous and causal. 

Now it is to have a model which gives an objective description of physical systems, independent of any acts of observation. Measuring apparatuses observes can then also be considered in equal footing with other physical systems, and process of observation themselves can be treated in detail without extraneous considerations.
