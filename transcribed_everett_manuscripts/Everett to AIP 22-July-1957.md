# Everett to AIP 22-July-1957.pdf

Hugh Everett, III  Institute for Defense Analyses  The Pentagon  Washington, D. C.  July 22, 1957  

Publication Manager  American Institute of Physics  335 East 45th Street  New York 17, New York  

Dear Sir:  

I am informed that the corrected galley proof of my article for the July Reviews of Modern Physics, which I sent you about two months ago, has been lost in the mails. I am therefore enclosing my second copy of the proof, with the required corrections in ink. The pencilled comments are notes I made before preparing the corrected proof which is lost, and should be ignored. The original M.S. is, of course, also lost.  

In case my order for reprints was lost too, I would like 200, with covers.  

Sincerely yours,  

Hugh Everett, III
